Large Group Skill Clinics

Sessions are strictly capped at 25 players to ensure focused and purposeful development. Each session is thoughtfully designed to maximize every second of ice time through intentional, high-repetition movement drills. We build from the foundation up—starting with focused skating skills—that serve as the essential base for advanced puck skills including puck handling, passing, and shooting. Players leave each session with a clear understanding of the skill being taught, why it's effective, and how it connects to overall game performance.

Small Group Sessions

Intensive, precision-focused training for 3 to 5 players per session, creating an environment for highly personalized coaching and immediate feedback. These 60-minute sessions are intentionally structured to isolate and refine technical mechanics through focused skating and puck drills with quality repetitions. With fewer players, I provide detailed, real-time corrections that accelerate mastery of complex skills, ensuring each player's development is thoughtful, purposeful, and impactful.
